Agriculture, Research & Impact

I am Amrish Yadav, an agriculture graduate and researcher from Nepal with a strong interest in agricultural research, vegetable production, technical efficiency, data analysis and sustainable agricultural development.

My academic journey has given me the opportunity to work closely with farmers and understand real-world challenges in agricultural production. I am particularly interested in research that can contribute to evidence-based decision-making and improved agricultural development.

My undergraduate research focused on the determinants of technical efficiency among vegetable farmers in Sarlahi District, using field-level data and Stochastic Frontier Analysis.
